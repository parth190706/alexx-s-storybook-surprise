# Alexx's Storybook Surprise

Build a complete, polished, mobile-first interactive birthday experience for Kriti aka Alexx with a pastel cream/blush/lavender storybook aesthetic and peaceful generative WebAudio soundtrack.

Include all 8 interconnected storybook chapters with full touch interactions:
1. Opening: Soft morning scene, floating particles, staged text ("hey kriti aka alexx..."), handwritten promise, "TAP TO START ✨" button that unlocks WebAudio (piano/pad/bell ambient chords + sound effects) with a working music toggle.
2. Chapter 1 (The Sky is Full of Trouble): Continuously rising and drifting balloons. Tapping pops them with particle bursts, pop sounds, and funny notes ("birthday tax.", "nice try 😭", etc.). Final special balloon bursts into stars to reveal "FIRST LITTLE THING" card.
3. Chapter 2 (One Perfect Shot): Twilight archery range. Physical touch/pointer pull-string bow and arrow that visibly arcs and flies towards targets. Hitting the glowing target opens a paper flower with the custom note and reward.
4. Chapter 3 (When the Clock Says Midnight): Magical central clock with draggable rotating hands. Moving the hands smoothly transitions the environment from evening to night (darker sky, rising moon, twinkling stars, fireflies, glowing windows). Reaching 12:00 triggers a midnight chime, starlight burst, and unlocks "THE MIDNIGHT WISH".
5. Chapter 4 (The Wish Wheel): Pastel birthday wish wheel. Swipe/drag to spin with rotational physics/momentum. 6 distinct wishes (Peaceful Year, A Surprise, A Quiet Win, A Little Luck, A Big Moment, Unlimited Sweetness). Allows 3 spins concluding with the special hidden wish ("THE ONE I ACTUALLY WANTED YOU TO GET").
6. Chapter 5 (Ice Cream Emergency): Falling ice creams (requestAnimationFrame loop). Draggable bowl catches them with bounce and sparkles until full, finishing with a giant ice cream, sprinkle burst, and reward.
7. Chapter 6 (Build Something Sweet): Cake-building table where layers (sponge, cream, sponge, frosting) drag and snap with sound and bounce, followed by strawberry/sprinkle/cherry decorations.
8. Chapter 7 (The Last Little Thing): Drag candles onto the cake, dim room, tap to light candles with warm flickering glow, and swipe across candles to blow them out with breeze particles and floating star.
9. Final Scene (For You, Alexx): Ambient night celebration containing subtle motifs from all chapters, revealing the full emotional line-by-line handwritten letter to sister Kriti aka Alexx, followed by soft confetti, warm glow, and sparkles.
10. Include 5-6 playful Easter egg secrets (tapping moon, stars, flowers, cake decorations). Ensure completely robust touch/pointer support for mobile, self-contained SVG/CSS illustrations, and clean, resilient WebAudio synthesis.
